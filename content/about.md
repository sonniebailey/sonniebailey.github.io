# About Buck

Buck Sandbox is 32, built like a fire hydrant, and has never once been described as subtle.

## The stillness

It started with a favour. An art tutor was short a model, Buck was short a rent payment, and forty minutes later he discovered he had a gift: he does not move. Not a twitch, not a sway, not a scratch. He has held a contrapposto through a fire alarm.

## The walking

Power walking found him during a knee injury that ruled out running. He entered a club event as a joke, won it, and stopped laughing. Three national titles later he trains most mornings along the waterfront, where he is a well known and frequently photographed local hazard.

## The lions

The lion taming is new. Buck completed his certification this year and now works with two rescued lions, Deborah and Kevin, both of whom he describes as "colleagues, not props". He is the first to admit he is early in this career. He is also the first to admit he is very good at it.

## What ties it together

Nerve. All three jobs come down to standing your ground while something large and unpredictable decides what to do about you. In the life room it is a hundred strangers with charcoal. On the course it is the last two kilometres. In the cage it is Kevin.
