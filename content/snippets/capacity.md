# Limited capacity

I work with clients one engagement at a time, with no ongoing arrangements to lock you in.

I keep my client load small: three or four people at most, and often fewer, depending on my other commitments. That means I can't take on everyone who gets in touch.

Past clients come first. If we've worked together before, get in touch whenever you're ready and I'll make room for you as soon as I can.

For new clients, I look for two things. First, that I can add real value; if I can't, I'll tell you straight away. Second, a good fit: I do my best work when I'm invested in your story and we enjoy the process.

So when you reach out, tell me a bit about yourself and what you're hoping for. A thoughtful message helps me see whether I'm the right person for you.
