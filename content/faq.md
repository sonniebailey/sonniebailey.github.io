# Questions people actually ask

## Are the lions real?

Yes. Deborah and Kevin are rescued lions, both retired from a closed facility overseas. They are not tame in the sense you are imagining. They are tame in the sense that Buck knows exactly what they are about to do.

## Is lion taming safe?

It is managed. Every booking begins with a site visit, every session runs with a second handler present, and there is a written plan for what happens if the answer to any question is no. Buck will decline a booking on the spot if the venue is wrong.

## Do you really not move at all when modelling?

Not for the length of the pose. Buck's record is four hours and ten minutes, broken only because the class ended.

## What is power walking, exactly?

Racewalking. One foot in contact with the ground at all times, front leg straight from contact until it passes under the body. Break either rule and you are running, and running gets you disqualified. It looks ridiculous and it is brutally hard.

## Can I book all three in one event?

You can. It is his favourite kind of booking and his most expensive.

## Is Buck Sandbox your real name?

It is now.
