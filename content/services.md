# What you can book me for

## Life modelling

Art schools, life drawing groups, private studios, sculpture classes. Poses from 30 seconds to four hours. Buck brings his own robe, his own heater, and a working knowledge of every anatomical term you are about to use incorrectly.

Rates are hourly, with a two hour minimum. Long poses across multiple sessions are welcome and preferred.

## Power walking

Appearances, exhibition walks, corporate fun runs where you would like someone to beat everybody at a walk. Also available for coaching: technique clinics, gait correction, and the specific business of walking fast without breaking into a run and being disqualified.

Buck has three national titles and holds the club record over 20 kilometres.

## Lion taming

The newest arm of the business. Buck works with two rescued lions, Deborah and Kevin, for events, film and photography, and educational demonstrations.

All work is fully insured and conducted to welfare standards that are considerably stricter than the ones applied to Buck himself. Bookings require a site visit first. There is no negotiation on this point.
